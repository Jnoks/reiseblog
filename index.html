FAQ

What's the story behind my travel blog?

This blog started as a way to remember. After my first long trip I noticed the details were fading faster than the photos could hold them, so I began writing everything down. What was meant as a private diary slowly turned into a place where other travelers come for honest, first-hand stories.

How do I plan, document, and share my travels?

Planning usually starts months ahead with a rough route, a budget, and a lot of reading. On the road I take notes every evening and shoot as I go, so nothing depends on memory later. The posts are written once I am back home, which keeps them honest rather than rushed.

How can brand, companies, or fellow creators collaborate with me or get in touch?

I work with brands, tourism boards, and fellow creators whose values match what I write about here. The easiest way to reach me is the contact form below – ideally with a short outline of what you have in mind and a rough timeline. Every serious request gets an answer, usually within a week.

How can readers support this blog and the work behind it?

The most valuable support is free: read the posts, share the ones you liked, and tell me what you would like to see next. If you want to go further, booking through the links you find here costs you nothing extra and helps cover hosting and travel expenses. Every message and every recommendation keeps this project going.
